🔒 Inventory Transaction Integrity
Every inventory change should be a discrete transaction. This prevents duplicate scans, stale edits, and unexplained quantity changes when multiple staff members work at once.
Receive
Adds stock and records the lot.
Pull / Use
Removes stock and records the case.
Return
Puts an unused item back into stock.
Quarantine
Blocks recalled or suspect lots from use.
